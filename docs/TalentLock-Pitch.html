The opportunity

Freelance hiring is still broken

Employers and freelancers still stitch together marketplaces, email threads, PDFs, calendars, and trust signals that do not talk to each other. TalentLock replaces that fragmented stack with one protected hiring loop.

What employers struggle with

  • Hours spent browsing mismatched profiles
  • No exclusivity once a freelancer is found
  • Handshake deals and weak contract coverage
  • Unclear identity and credential trust
  • Spend and hiring outcomes scattered across tools

What freelancers struggle with

  • Race-to-the-bottom marketplaces
  • Ghosted proposals and unpaid discovery time
  • No durable proof of exclusivity or quality
  • Manual rate negotiation and weak deal flow
  • Little visibility into earnings intelligence
The answer

One platform. Full hiring lifecycle.

Why TalentLock wins

It is not another job board. It is infrastructure for professional freelance engagement — matching, exclusivity, verification, legal protection, and operational intelligence designed as one product, not five disconnected tools.

Product pillars

Built around trust and exclusivity

AI Talent Matching

Employers describe the need; GPT-powered matching recommends freelancers with explanations for skills, rate, and availability.

Exclusive Bookings

Confirmed bookings lock talent availability and show a Lock badge — exclusivity is the core market differentiator.

Verified Identity

Freelancer Aadhaar/ID verification and employer business document verification create mutual trust before work starts.

AI Legal Agreements

Generate or upload contracts, score health, summarise in plain English, redline, e-sign, and download certified PDFs.

Cruise Mode & TalentSearch

Rules-based AI outreach: freelancers auto-express interest in jobs; employers auto-reach matching talent via DMs.

Discovery → Brief → Book

Schedule discovery meetings with calendar guests, then receive AI meeting briefs before confirmed calls.

Multi-Currency Hiring

Country-derived currency, dual display in Talent Vault, and frozen booking FX for clean cross-border agreements.

Enterprise Teams

Shared shortlists, invites, team analytics, and unlimited enterprise capacity for high-volume hiring orgs.
